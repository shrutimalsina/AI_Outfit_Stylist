# Use the original outfit splits with Re-PolyVore images

The available metadata and images join by outfit ID and item index. Their
coverage supports only 1,373 complete outfits, so the seed-42 experiment
uses groups with at least two verified images and records original group size.
The study preserves the original outfit splits. Its candidate catalog includes
held-out items, and product identities can repeat across outfit-scoped IDs.
The [evaluation protocol](../evaluation-protocol.md) explains the implications.
